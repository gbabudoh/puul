- ✅ **Set privacy** (Private or Public)
- ✅ **Create button** → Saves and shows success message

### 4. 👤 Profile Screen
Click profile icon to access:
- ✅ **Profile header** with avatar and email
- ✅ **Stats card** showing PUULs, Connections, Photos
- ✅ **Edit Profile** button
- ✅ **Menu items**:
  - Connections
  - Notifications
  - Privacy

  - Help & Support
  - About
  - Logout (with confirmation)
